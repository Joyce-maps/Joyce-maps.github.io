# Joyce-maps.github.io

An Expanding Universe: the Languages of Joyce's Works

    Check out animated maps of how Joyce's <i>Dubliners</i> and <i>Ulysses</i> spread across languages over time. 
    Made by UAntwerp students in the Masters of Digital Text Analysis Yukiko Tominaga and Matthew Bohez, based on 
    Patrick O'Neill and Kris Peeters (2026). <i>Joyce Translated</i>. Owl Press | University of Antwerp Press.
    (c) Kris Peeters, University of Antwerp, <a href="https://www.uantwerpen.be/JJTC">James Joyce in Translation Centre</a>.
